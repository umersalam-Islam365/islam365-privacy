Privacy Policy

Islam 365 · Last updated: September 2026
In short: Islam 365 does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. There is no user account, no login, and no advertising or analytics tracking of any kind. Everything the app remembers about you — your reading position, bookmarks, and settings — stays on your own device.

Who we are

Islam 365 is an Islamic lifestyle app developed by Umer Salam, providing Quran reading, Hadith collections, prayer times, Qibla direction, and daily Islamic content. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it's used.

Location data

Islam 365 uses your device's location for two features:

  • Prayer times — your coordinates are sent directly to the AlAdhan prayer-times API (aladhan.com) to calculate accurate prayer timings for your area.
  • Qibla direction — your coordinates, combined with your device's compass, calculate the direction to the Kaaba in Makkah.
Your location is used only at the moment you request prayer times or Qibla direction. It is not stored by Islam 365, not linked to any personal identity, and not shared with anyone other than the AlAdhan API needed to perform the calculation. If you prefer not to share location, you can enter your city manually instead — this is always available as an alternative.

Notifications

Islam 365 schedules local Adhan (prayer call) notifications directly on your device based on your calculated prayer times. These notifications are generated and managed entirely on your device — no notification data is sent to or from any server.

Data stored on your device

The following information is saved locally on your phone, using standard Android app storage, and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your last-read page in the Quran (per reading mode)
  • Bookmarks you've added
  • Your chosen translation language, reciter, font size, and other display preferences
  • A local cache of Quran translations, so they load faster and work offline after the first time you view them
  • Your manually entered city/country, if you chose that instead of GPS location

This data is removed automatically if you uninstall the app, or you can clear it anytime via your device's Settings → Apps → Islam 365 → Storage → Clear Data.

Third-party services used

Islam 365 fetches religious content from the following external, publicly available sources. These requests do not include any personal information beyond what's technically necessary (e.g., your location coordinates for prayer-time calculation, as described above):

  • AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) — prayer times and Hijri calendar data
  • AlQuran Cloud API — Quran ayah translations
  • fawazahmed0 Hadith API (via jsDelivr CDN) — Hadith text and translations
  • mp3quran.net — Quran audio recitation streaming

Each of these is a separate, independent service with its own privacy practices. Islam 365 does not control, and is not responsible for, how these third parties handle any data sent to them as part of a normal API request (such as your IP address, which is inherent to any internet connection).

What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account or log in.
  • We don't collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier.
  • We don't show advertisements.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share any data, because we don't collect any to begin with.

Children's privacy

Islam 365 does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app requires no account and stores no personal data, it is safe for use by users of any age under a parent or guardian's guidance where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated in the future, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how Islam 365 handles information, you can reach out via the contact details listed on the app's Play Store page.
